# Presentation Outline: SawadeeBot
**Duration:** 10 Minutes + 5 Minutes Q&A

## Slide 1: Title & Introduction (30s)
- Project Name: SawadeeBot
- Team Members
- Goal: Revolutionizing Thai language school student support.

## Slide 2: The Problem (1m)
- High volume of repetitive FAQs.
- Lost leads during non-business hours.
- High cost of manual human support for simple tasks.

## Slide 3: The Solution - AI Persona & Dashboard (1m)
- Introducing SawadeeBot.
- Persona: Warm, polite, "Thai Hospitality".
- Capabilities: Info retrieval, Booking, Support Ticketing.
- **Admin Oversight:** Three-Tier Dashboard for real-time monitoring.

## Slide 4: Why AI? (Justification) (1.5m)
- **Data:** Structured course catalogs.
- **Pattern:** Repeatable support workflows.
- **Business Value:** 24/7 conversion, cost reduction.
- **Feasibility:** Powered by Gemini 3.1 Pro/Flash.

## Slide 5: System Workflow (2m)
- **Visual Diagram:** User -> Web UI -> Gemini (Context Grounding) -> Firebase (Action Storage).
- Explaining the "Structured Actions" concept.
- **Hybrid Support:** The "Admin Handover" mechanism.

## Slide 6: Technology Stack (1m)
- Frontend: Next.js 15, Tailwind, Shadcn.
- Backend: Vercel Edge, Firebase.
- Brain: Google Gemini 3.1 Pro/Flash via OpenRouter.
- UX: Mobile-optimized with responsive scrollable navigation.

## Slide 7: Ethics & Risks (1.5m)
- Mitigating Hallucinations (Knowledge Grounding).
- Data Privacy (Secure Firebase storage).
- Bias Management (Persona constraints).
- When AI is NOT used (Refunds/Complex disputes).

## Slide 8: AI Usage Log (0.5m)
- How we used Antigravity to build, debug, and document the project.
- Prompt-to-Product workflow.

## Slide 9: Demo (Optional / Recorded) (1m)
- Quick video or live showing of booking a class.

## Slide 10: Conclusion & Q&A (0.5m)
- Future Work: Multi-lingual support, WhatsApp integration.
- Open for questions.
